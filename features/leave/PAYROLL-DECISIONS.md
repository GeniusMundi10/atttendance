# Decisions needed before payroll calculations

The Attendance and Leave Policy and HR flow workbook leave several pay-affecting rules open or in conflict. The examples below are illustrations only; no payroll behavior is activated from them.

1. **Late and short-hours deductions:** The policy says the first three occasions in a month are allowed, then each later occurrence is 0.25 absent day. The workbook describes the same late threshold. Example: 20 scheduled workdays, 4 late arrivals and 4 short-hours days. If each counter is independent, that is 2 × 0.25 = 0.5 absent day. If both share one monthly allowance, the 8 events produce 5 deductions = 1.25 days. Should late and short-hours have separate or shared three-event limits? Are qualifying corrections and approved field/remote days counted?
2. **Short leave:** Policy: 8 hours total, max 2 hours at a time. Workbook: four 2-hour short leaves per month and classifies them as unpaid. Example: one 2-hour short leave in February leaves either 6 hours for the year/overall pool, or 6 hours for February and a fresh 8 hours in March under monthly reset. Confirm whether the allowance resets monthly, whether unused hours carry forward, and whether short leave is unpaid. If unpaid, confirm the payroll divisor (scheduled shift hours, actual working days, or another basis).
3. **Partial-month paid-leave entitlement:** Policy grants one paid leave per month but says nothing about joining/leaving mid-month. Example: an employee starts on the 16th of a 30-day month. Should they receive a full 1-day credit, a prorated 0.5-day credit by calendar days, a workday-prorated fraction, or no credit until the next month? Confirm rounding and the treatment of a final partial month.
4. **Holiday and weekly-off pay:** Workbook lists Sunday as weekly off and includes public holidays; policy does not define their payable-unit treatment. Example: an employee has 22 scheduled weekdays, 4 Sundays and 1 public holiday in a month, and takes 2 unpaid days. Should regular monthly pay count non-workdays as paid by default, and what divisor determines the 2-day deduction? If the employee works on a Sunday/public holiday, is there extra pay, a compensatory day, or ordinary attendance only?
5. **Restricted-holiday eligibility conflict:** Policy grants one optional holiday per year. Workbook says one RH credit after completing three months of service. Example: a new employee takes Krishna Janmashtami before completing three months. Should they be eligible from their start date or only after the three-month service threshold? Confirm whether the annual one-day allowance resets by calendar year or service anniversary.

Until ELT confirms these choices, systems may record schedules, approvals and attendance facts, but must not translate these rules into salary deductions or payable units.
